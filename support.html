Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone only. There's no account, so nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and progress doesn't move between phones.

When are new missions, collectibles and cheats added?
Lists are added as they're confirmed, starting on the game's release day. The app downloads updates by itself when you open it or pull down to refresh on the home screen; you don't need to update the app.

I don't see the latest items.
Make sure you're online, then pull down on the home screen to refresh. Updates can take up to 10 minutes to reach everyone.

How do I hide spoilers?
Open Settings (the gear icon) and turn spoilers on or off separately for story missions, side quests and collectibles.

How do I reset my progress?
Settings → Reset progress. This can't be undone.

Can I switch between PlayStation and Xbox cheat codes?
Yes. Use the PS / Xbox toggle on the Cheat codes card. The choice is remembered on cheat detail pages too.

Why do I see ads, and how do I control tracking?
Ads keep Vice Tracker free. On iPhone you can change tracking anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In Europe and the UK you choose your ad consent when the app first opens. Details are in the privacy policy.
