Vice Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

Vice Tracker is an unofficial companion app for tracking game progress. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

No account, and your progress stays on your phone

Vice Tracker has no sign-up or account. Your ticked missions, favorites and settings (such as spoiler and platform choices) are stored only on your device. We do not receive or store them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Mission and cheat list

To keep its mission and cheat list current, the app downloads a data file from GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any website, GitHub may log your IP address and basic request details when the file is downloaded. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Advertising

Vice Tracker is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, when permitted)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any ads load, the app shows Google's consent form so you can accept or decline personalized ads. You can change your choice later by reinstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Tracking on iPhone. The app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, Google's consent tools let you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

Sharing your progress

If you use "Share progress", the app creates an image on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose an app to share it with.

Children

Vice Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to content suitable for teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App launch statistics

To count how many people use each version of the app, each time the app starts it sends a small launch report to Expo Insights, a service of 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo). The report contains a random ID created by the app when it was installed (not your advertising ID, and not linked to your name or account), the app version, your platform (iOS or Android) and its OS version. As with any request, Expo also receives your IP address. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics

To learn which parts of the app are used most, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC. It records when you open or close a group of cards on the home screen (with the group's name) and when you tap an ad (with where the ad was shown, such as the home screen, a card group, or a mission or cheat page). Firebase also collects standard usage information such as an app-instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and session activity. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

The app does not include any other analytics or crash-reporting tools.
